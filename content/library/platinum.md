## The price

On Yahoo Finance's futures prices, platinum averaged about **US\$2,030 an ounce in March 2008**. It then spent most of
the next twelve years falling: about **US\$860** in December 2015 and about **US\$760** in March 2020, roughly
**60% below** the 2008 level. A weaker rand cushioned this for South African producers, whose costs are in rand: in
rand terms the price in early 2016 was within about 10% of its mid-2008 level (chart).

## The miners

A mine's costs barely fall when the price does, so a falling price squeezes profits hard. On the app's data,
**Impala Platinum (Implats)** shares, including dividends, fell **95%** from their peak in May 2008 to their low in
August 2018, while the Top 40 index rose about 65% over the same period (price only).

## The 2014 strike

On **23 January 2014**, almost **70,000** platinum mine workers at Impala Platinum, Anglo American Platinum and Lonmin,
represented by the Association of Mineworkers and Construction Union (AMCU), went on strike for higher wages. The strike
lasted **five months**, until a deal was signed on **24 June 2014**. According to South African History Online, the
three companies lost about **R24.1 billion** of revenue and workers about **R10.6 billion** in wages, and the strike took
around **440,000 ounces** of platinum out of production.

## And then it turned

Commodity cycles run both ways. On Yahoo Finance's data, platinum reached a record of about **US\$2,850** in January
2026, and Implats shares rose more than tenfold from their 2018 low (app data). An investor who sold out near the
bottom missed that; one heavily invested from 2008 had to sit through a 95% fall first.

## Who was hurt

Mineworkers, who lost about R10.6 billion in wages during the strike; shareholders in platinum miners;
and South African funds with large resources weightings.

## The lesson for a fund builder

- **One-commodity sectors are cyclical.** Their profits swing with the price of what they dig up.
- **Costs and labour matter as well as prices.** A strike can stop production for months.
- **Check how much of your fund depends on one sector.** The app's sector look-through shows it, and the resources-heavy
  insight flags a fund above 25% in resources.
